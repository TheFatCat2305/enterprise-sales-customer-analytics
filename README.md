# Enterprise Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Business Problem
Sales and marketing teams often lack a clear, data-driven view of:
- Which customer segments drive the majority of revenue
- Which customers are at risk of churn
- Where cross-sell opportunities exist and how to prioritise them

This project addresses how enterprise teams can use customer and sales data
to improve revenue focus, retention, and basket size.

---

## 🛠 What I Did
- Designed an end-to-end enterprise BI dashboard using Power BI
- Built an executive reporting layer for high-level performance monitoring
- Applied RFM segmentation to classify customers by value and behaviour
- Developed rule-based cross-sell analysis to identify product bundling opportunities
- Created business-focused KPIs to quantify revenue concentration and uplift potential

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages
### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Revenue, Customer Count, Average Order Value
- Revenue trends and high-level performance indicators

### 2. Customer Segmentation (RFM)
- Identification of Champions, Loyal, At Risk, and Lost customers
- Revenue concentration and retention risk analysis

### 3. Cross-sell Analysis
- Product combinations with highest business impact
- Cross-sell adoption rate and unrealised revenue opportunity
- Segment-level cross-sell recommendations

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- Top 20% of customers contribute 61% of total revenue
- 49% of customers generate 80.61% of revenue
- At Risk customers contribute over 21% of revenue
- 46.82% of orders present cross-sell opportunities
- Estimated unrealised cross-sell revenue: $166.70K

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Prioritise retention for At Risk and Cannot Lose Them segments
- Apply selective engagement for low-value, high-volume segments
- Focus cross-sell campaigns on high-confidence product bundles
